Essential Gas Stove Safety & Care: 5 Golden Rules

1. Clean Burner Caps Weekly: Carbonized milk spills and oil residues clog miniature brass burner holes. Wash cool brass caps in warm water with dish soap, and use a thin safety wire (never wooden toothpicks) to unclog the ports.

2. Perform Monthly Soap-Water Leak Checks: Mix liquid soap with water and dab it around your regulator neck and hose connections. If bubbles expand, gas is escaping! Switch off immediately and call for repair.

3. Replace LPG Rubber Pipes Every 2 Years: Rubber hoses harden and develop microscopic stress fractures over time. Always insist on steel-braided ISI Suraksha pipes with valid manufacturing dates.

4. Watch for Flame Color: A healthy flame burns crisp blue. Persistent orange or yellow tips indicate incomplete combustion, which wastes gas and produces soot on utensils.

5. Never Force Stiff Knobs: Forcing a jammed knob breaks the internal brass spindle and can cause irreversible valve leaks. A technician can clean and lubricate it in 10 minutes.
