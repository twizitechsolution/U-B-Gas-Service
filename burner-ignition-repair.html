First-Click Ignition Restoration

Auto-ignition failure is the single most common frustration in modular kitchens. Whether it is battery-operated (DC 1.5V) or AC electric pulse ignition, our technicians carry spark modules, replacement microswitches, ceramic ignition pins, and original brass burner caps.

Common Auto-Ignition Troubles We Fix:

  • ⚡ No Spark at All: Depleted battery compartment, oxidized contacts, or failed pulse generator module.
  • ⚡ Weak or Orange Spark: Moisture or food spill carbon buildup on the ceramic ignition electrode.
  • ⚡ Continuous Rapid Clicking: Microswitch stuck under knob or moisture short-circuit in the wiring loom.
  • ⚡ Only One Burner Failing: Broken spark lead or misaligned electrode air gap.
