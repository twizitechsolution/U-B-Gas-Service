Quick Troubleshooting

Common Stove & Hob Problems We Fix

Identify your gas appliance issue below for instant diagnosis and direct WhatsApp dispatch.

🍳

Low Flame or Slow Cooking

Carbon soot or oil deposits block brass burner jets and venturi pipes, cutting cooking heat by over 60%.

⏱️ Fix Time: 25 Mins
⚡

Auto Ignition Spark Failure

Knob clicks endlessly without igniting or fails to produce any spark due to a damaged pulse generator or switch.

⏱️ Fix Time: 35 Mins
⚠️

Pungent Gas Smell / Leakage

Dangerous odor caused by aged rubber tube cracks, faulty regulator O-rings, or defective gas cock spindle joints.

🚨 Priority Dispatch 📞 Emergency Call
🟡

Yellow Flame / Black Utensils

Improper air-to-gas ratio causes incomplete fuel combustion, wasting expensive LPG and coating pots in black soot.

⏱️ Fix Time: 25 Mins
🔒

Stiff or Jammed Control Knob

Spilled food gravies solidify inside the internal spindle, making knobs difficult or impossible to turn safely.

⏱️ Fix Time: 20 Mins
💥

Hissing Sound or Flame Flashback

Loud whistling, popping, or flame burning inside the mixing tube due to loose burner caps or damaged nozzle injectors.

⏱️ Fix Time: 30 Mins
