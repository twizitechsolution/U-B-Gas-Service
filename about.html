About U&B Gas Service Pune

Founded with one clear mission: make gas stove and hob repair in Pune genuinely safe, transparent, and available right at your doorstep.

Our Origin Story

Serving Pune's Kitchens Since 2016

U&B Gas Service started in Pune when our founding gas safety technicians noticed families frequently suffering from delayed, low-quality repairs. Traditional roadside workshops demanded customers bring their heavy appliances to distant shops, charging high transport fees and using duplicate parts.

We transformed the model into 100% Doorstep Service: upfront transparent estimates before starting, genuine OEM spare parts, and mandatory soap-solution leak testing. Today, we have successfully completed 5,200+ doorstep repairs across Pune & PCMC.
