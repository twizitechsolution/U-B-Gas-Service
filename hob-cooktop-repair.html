Precision Repair for Premium Glass Hobs

Built-in hobs require specialist technical knowledge due to sealed under-counter manifolds, flame-failure safety devices (FFD), Italian brass burners, and tempered glass surfaces. Our technicians are factory-trained on luxury modular kitchen systems.

Cracked Hob Glass Warning:

If your toughened hob glass has a crack, stop using it immediately. Continued heat causes thermal stress and dangerous shattering. Call us for replacement glass.
