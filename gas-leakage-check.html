Smelling Gas Right Now? Immediate Action Protocol:

✅ WHAT YOU MUST DO IMMEDIATELY:

  • Turn OFF the cylinder regulator switch completely.
  • Open all kitchen windows and exterior doors for ventilation.
  • Extinguish all open flames (diyas, agarbatti, cigarettes).
  • Step into the open balcony or outside if smell is strong.

❌ WHAT YOU MUST NEVER DO:

  • DO NOT turn ON or OFF any electric switches (sparks ignite gas).
  • DO NOT light matchsticks or lighters to search for the leak.
  • DO NOT use cellphones directly inside the kitchen room.
  • DO NOT operate exhaust fans if smelling gas.
Certified 5-Point Safety Check

Comprehensive End-to-End Pipeline Inspection

LPG is intentionally odorized with Ethyl Mercaptan so you can detect even minute leaks. Our technicians inspect the complete gas flow circuit:

  • 🔍 1. Cylinder Valve & Rubber Bushing: Check for damaged O-ring seals inside the cylinder neck.
  • 🔍 2. Pressure Regulator Joint: Test regulator locking mechanism and seal integrity.
  • 🔍 3. Suraksha LPG Hose: Inspect for age cracks, rodent bite marks, and loose safety jubilee clamps.
  • 🔍 4. Stove Inward Nozzle & Internal Manifold: Leak test under active gas pressure.
  • 🔍 5. Valve Stem Sealing: Ensure rotary burner knobs do not leak in Off or Sim positions.
